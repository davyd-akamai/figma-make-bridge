Breadcrumbs

Location trail from the root to the current page. Earlier items are links followed by a "/" separator; the last item is the bold current page, optionally renamable inline (pencil on hover, text field with confirm/cancel). Any item can carry a BETA/NEW marker.

Trail lengths

1 to 5 items, matching Figma's swatches. Hover a non-last item to see the underline.

Interactive — navigation, badge, rename

Click "Compute" or "Instances" to navigate. Hover the last item and click the pencil to rename: Enter or ✓ confirms, Escape or ✕ cancels. "Instances" carries a BETA badge.
