Tooltip

Short text label that appears next to a control on hover or keyboard focus. Wraps the control it describes, flips to a side that fits the viewport, and closes with Esc.

Placements

Hover a button, or Tab to it. placement is the side of the trigger the tooltip sits on; Figma names its swatches after the side the arrow is on, so placement="top" is Figma's "Bottom" swatch. arrow={false} is Figma's "None".

Text, icon triggers and flipping

Text wraps once it reaches 240px. An icon-only control is a good fit, and must be a focusable button with an aria-label. A tooltip flips to the opposite side when its preferred side doesn't fit the viewport: scroll this page so the last trigger is near the top edge and hover it.
