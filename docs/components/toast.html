Toast

Floating notification fixed to the bottom-right of the viewport. Four types, auto-dismisses after 5 seconds, or immediately via the ×. Visibility is controlled by the consumer.

Types

Click a button to show that toast at the bottom-right of the window. It slides in, auto-dismisses after 5 seconds, and can be closed early with the ×. Only one toast should be open at a time (no stacking).

No auto-dismiss

duration={0} disables the timer; the toast stays until the × is clicked.
