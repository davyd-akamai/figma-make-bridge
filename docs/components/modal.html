Modal

Centered dialog with a title, text body and right-aligned actions over a dimmed backdrop. One fixed 459px width. Focus-trapped, closes on the close button, backdrop click, or Esc.

Playground

The footer is a plain slot for library Buttons, and the description is just children. The icon, the footer and the overlay-click behavior are all optional.
